# Elo (`/elo`)

Visitor mode: **Operate** (a data page; desktop goes wide with the table and chart side by side). A friend checking who's actually good right now, how the season has moved, and how a team has fared over the years.

## Scope
Production Next.js route in `web/`. Ratings from the project's own Elo engine (`src/elo.py`, tuned settings in `data/processed/elo_params.json`) over every result in the odds sheet since 2009, generated by `web/scripts/build_elo_sample.py`; no calculation in the page. Four parts: ratings table (17 teams: rank, rating, change since last round, rating at season start), season chart (a faint line per team, tap teams to highlight in their colours, your favourite highlighted by default, names at line ends instead of a legend), season picker 2009-2026, and a team view (one team over recent seasons, its biggest rises and falls labelled by the match). A short plain explanation of what a rating gap means. 2026 runs to the sample's current results (after Round 9).

## States
Current season mid-way, a completed past season, a team view, a season where a team didn't exist (Dolphins before 2023), no team highlighted.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The competition as a ladder of strength that moves every week: a straight table you can scan, beside the season drawn as a bundle of faint lines where the teams you care about light up in their own colours. Refuses the power-rankings listicle and the rainbow spaghetti chart.
OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md unchanged: the chart system recorded from /odds (2px lines, ringed end dots, hairline grid, crosshair tooltip, table view, measured width), but series here are team colours (team bar colours, clash rule applied, at most three highlighted), the rest of the field in ink-3 at low opacity. Team badges, tabular Saira figures, hairline rows. Straight voice: no banter.
STORY: See this week's order and who moved, pick a team or two to see their season's path against the field, open a team to see its years and the games that swung it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390px: "Elo ratings" heading with "After Round 9, 2026" and the season picker; one plain line on what a rating gap means; the ratings table's top rows (rank, badge, team, rating, change).
