## Forward feature selection (linear models)

Greedy selection from the 45 base + player features on walk-forward CV, adding a feature only if it improves the score in every CV season and the pooled score by at least 0.001 (log loss, `home_win`) or 0.01 points (MAE, `total`). The win and margin models use the `home_win` selection, the total model the `total` one; Model A adds the opening odds. LightGBM uses the full set.
